about=Adds a "Sort group layers ..." entry to the right-click menu of layer groups. Layers in one or several selected groups can be sorted by geometry type (points, lines, polygons, other) and by layer name (natural, case-insensitive). Sub groups can be placed at the top or bottom (sorted alphabetically), and sorting can optionally be applied recursively.
